Senior Safety Guide:
Barangay Culiat

A dedicated resource for our elders in Barangay Culiat. We are committed to providing a secure environment, continuing the legacy of community service through easy-to-use digital tools and local support.

Quick Action

How to Report an Incident

Simple steps to get help when you need it most. Our system is designed for clarity and speed for all Barangay Culiat residents.

touch_app

1. Press the Help Button

Locate the large red button on your screen or the physical alarm in your area.

record_voice_over

2. Describe the Situation

Speak clearly or type a simple message. Tell us what's happening and where you are.

emergency_share

3. Help is on the way

Stay calm and remain where you are. Our nearest response unit is already moving to you.

Community Safety Tips

Advice from your neighbors and the Tandang Sora local council.

Keep Emergency Numbers Handy

Post the local hotline near your phone or save it on speed dial for immediate assistance.

Be Aware of Scams

Never give out your personal information over the phone to strangers claiming to be from the government.

Stay Connected

Join our local 'Bantay Senior' group to keep each other informed and looked after by the community.
